# Brand - RatLevel

Status: active

RatLevel is a premium dark-first fitness game. The brand should feel like a clean gym membership app fused with RPG progression: direct, sharp, energetic, and never cluttered.

## Palette

- Background: `#0D1117`
- Surface: `#161B22`
- Elevated Surface: `#1C2430`
- Border: `#30363D`
- Primary Blue: `#2F80FF`
- Success: `#22C55E`
- Warning: `#F59E0B`
- Danger: `#EF4444`
- XP Purple: `#8B5CF6`
- Text Primary: `#F8FAFC`
- Text Secondary: `#B6C2D1`

## Typography

Use bold, modern sans typography. Headings are compact and confident. Numbers, levels, XP, and ranks should feel game-like through weight, spacing, and hierarchy rather than decorative fonts.

## Voice

Short, active, progression-oriented copy. Users do not simply complete tasks; they earn XP, preserve streaks, unlock badges, climb ranks, and improve character stats.
